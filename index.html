The Signal Engine

The signals-to-roadmap framework that makes the plans comparable and the roadmap evidence-driven. Five layers — from raw data to a ranked roadmap.

Layer 1 Data inputs

Competitive and external data answer a question telemetry can't: “is our attrition an app problem or a market problem in this region?”

InputExamplesCadence

Layer 2 → 3 Cohort scores → interventions interactive

Scores are computed per cohort (role × shift × region × tenure band), never per individual — statistically saner, and the right privacy posture. Move the sliders to simulate a cohort’s state and watch the decision layer select intervention families.

Presets:

Decision layer output

No thresholds tripped — this cohort reads healthy. The engine stays quiet: interventions compete for a scarce attention budget.

Incentive compliance guardrail: gift cards to employees are taxable wages, incentive fairness across protected classes must be auditable, and reward mechanics must never read as pay-for-surveillance. Every incentive arm ships with an HR/legal review and an equity audit.

Layer 4 Learning layer — contextual Multi-Armed Bandits

With dozens of cohorts × multiple intervention arms, classic A/B testing is too slow and wastes exposure on losing arms. A contextual bandit (Thompson sampling) treats cohort features — role, shift, region, tenure — as context and learns the best intervention per cohort while still exploring. West-coast overnight stockers may respond to different incentives than East-coast stockers — the bandit finds out, and surfaces the pattern for humans to interrogate.

Layer 5 Roadmapping surface interactive

Every candidate roadmap item is scored by expected minutes returned + expected retention lift × confidence, against build cost. Adjust the weights — the ranking recomputes. Roadmap reviews stop being opinion tournaments; they become “here’s what the signals rank, and here’s where I’m overriding the model and why.”

Illustrative candidates with hypothetical scores — the real dashboard is fed by Layers 2–4. Score = (minutes-returned × weight + retention-lift × weight) × confidence ÷ build cost.
