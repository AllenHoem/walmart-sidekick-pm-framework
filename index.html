The reward ladder — real rewards, three grant mechanics

Compliance, said out loud: gift cards, bonuses, and stock are taxable compensation — payroll integration, not an app feature. Sweepstakes carry their own legal regime (no-consideration entry, state rules). Merit and promotion nominations flow through existing HR calibration — the app nominates with evidence, humans decide. Every tier ships with an equity audit, and reward mechanics must never read as pay-for-surveillance.

Layer 4 Learning layer — contextual Multi-Armed Bandits

With dozens of cohorts × multiple intervention arms, classic A/B testing is too slow and wastes exposure on losing arms. A contextual bandit (Thompson sampling) treats cohort features — role, shift, region, tenure — as context and learns the best intervention per cohort while still exploring. West-coast overnight stockers may respond to different incentives than East-coast stockers — the bandit finds out, and surfaces the pattern for humans to interrogate.

Layer 5 Roadmapping surface
